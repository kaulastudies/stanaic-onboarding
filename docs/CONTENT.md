# Content sources and scope

Reviewed on 30 October 2026.

- Official website supplied by the project contributor: https://stanaic.org/
- Search-indexed official homepage: https://stanaic.com/ — describes AI certification, governance, and enterprise talent training. Contact shown there is info@stanaic.org.
- The .org page returned an IP-validation/WAF screen to the automated reader. The .com live fetch was also unavailable. These limitations mean the full current commercial terms and official role catalogue have **not** been verified.

Accordingly, the portal contains neutral process wording only: invite, submit details, review, and notify. It links to the official website. It does not assert fees, universally free access, honorary status, accreditation, automatic certification, employment, compensation, or legal guarantees.

The administrator supplies the role or programme agreed for each invitation. Historical invitation titles populate the suggestion list; they are operational records, not a published catalogue. Staff access roles describe software permissions only.

Rama Chandra contributes this onboarding module in gratitude for STANAIC's understanding and support. His individual invitation terms do not define terms for other applicants. His personal circumstances are not part of the public application copy.

Future additions must use STANAIC-approved source documents for pricing, agreements, programme descriptions, document signatures, certificates, or public directory publication. This initial version does not invent those policies.
