A traceable learning journey

Completion is earned.
Credentials are checkable.

The existing AIG programme displays eight modules and an 80% final pass threshold. This demo uses an original short lesson and a five-question sample assessment.

Study & practice

Resume lessons, complete knowledge checks, and record progress against a specific programme version.

Assessment & review

Final assessments run on the server in production. Applied work requiring judgement stays with an instructor or assessor.

Issue & verify

A registrar issues only after the required evidence and approval. Public verification shows status, scope, dates, and revocation.

A Certificate of Completion is distinct from a professional certification, fellowship appointment, enterprise assurance opinion, or regulatory approval.
