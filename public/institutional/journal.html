Proposed editorial workflow

Every decision has an owner.

SubmissionEditorial screeningDouble-blind reviewRevision & decisionProduction & publication

Authors

Submit metadata, declarations, a separately anonymised manuscript, and supporting evidence. Track revisions and respond to reviewers.

Editors & reviewers

Check scope and conflicts, assign appropriate reviewers, evaluate evidence, and record the editorial decision independently of sponsorship.

Production

Approve proofs, confirm licensing, prepare accessible files, register identifiers when eligible, and maintain corrections and retractions.
